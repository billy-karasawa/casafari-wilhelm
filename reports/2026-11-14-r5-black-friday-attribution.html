Black Friday attribution: first two days (R5)

Prepared by Wilhelm for Billy, Sat 14 Nov 2026. Source: BigQuery casafari-cloud.casafari_platform, nightly export of 14 Nov 02:30 UTC. This covers sign-ups from 12 Nov 00:00 to 14 Nov 02:30 UTC. Internal and test accounts are excluded. Read-only queries; the tables are about 100 KB, so cost is negligible.

Headline. Black Friday brought 400 tagged new organisations in 2 days, about 10× the normal rate of about 20 sign-ups a day.
  • By channel: banners 304 (app 252, mobile 52), email 1 95, untagged code 1.
  • Activation: 15% have run a query so far, about 85% of them through MCP.
  • Revenue: no paid conversions yet. No paid plan, no card on file, no invoice.
  • Reconciliation: Mila's agent's figures check out (456 total, 87 email, 299 banners). HubSpot's "16" is a reporting problem: HubSpot holds the tags for 75% of these sign-ups.

Reconciliation

  • Bot post of 389, and Mila's agent's 456 (13 Nov, about 20:18 UTC): BigQuery at that exact time shows 388 Black Friday-tagged and 457 total new organisations. Exact match. The 389 counts tagged sign-ups only; the 456 includes untagged ones.
  • By end of day the counts had grown to 473 total, 95 from email (agent said 87) and 304 from banners (agent said 299). The 2–9% differences are sign-ups after the post.
  • HubSpot shows 16: wrong as a count. The platform wrote the tracking tags to HubSpot contacts for 307 of 408 tagged sign-ups (75%). The "16" must come from a report that filters on something else: deals, lifecycle stage or hs_analytics_source. Fix the HubSpot report, or report from BigQuery. 101 sign-ups never reached HubSpot, including the 4 R-codes.

What it means

  • Reach worked; conversion hasn't started. Black Friday sign-ups activate at about half the same-day rate of organic sign-ups (15% vs 33%). The baseline cohort reached 53% with more days, so expect this to rise. Watch it in R5 runs after email 2 (19 Nov).
  • Payment comes later. The token bonus (+30% to 23 Nov, +25% 24–30 Nov) only applies on a top-up. With no paid conversion yet, no bonus tokens have been granted.
  • Banners outperform email in volume (304 vs 95), so in-app users are the main audience. Email converts to usage slightly better (20% vs 16%). Mobile banner users barely activate (6%).
  • No country split: the billing country comes from Stripe, which only applies after a card payment. A country view needs another source, such as member email domain or HubSpot contact country.

Open questions

  • 4 sign-ups with random "R…" codes and no tracking tags (e.g. RGW64G1BY9XYC). They aren't flagged as client or staff referrals. They're probably personal referral links or partner links. Kate or the platform team can confirm.
  • 1 sign-up with BLACKFRIDAY30 but no tracking tags: the code was typed or the link was shared directly.
  • Are any of these 400 sign-ups existing web-app clients? That needs a match by domain or email to HubSpot companies. Not done here, because the platform tables hold no emails.

Method

Tables: accounts (excluding is_internal and accounts flagged internal on any night), account_attribution (UTM tracking tags, promotion code, hubspot_written_at), usage_daily (calls, billed units, channel), invoices (status paid). Channel = utm_campaign "black-friday…": email if utm_medium=email; mobile if the source is casafari-mobile or mobile-app; otherwise app banner. Days are Lisbon dates.
