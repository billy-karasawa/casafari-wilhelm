Slack trend scan, 2 to 13 November 2026

Prepared by Wilhelm for Billy, 13 Nov 2026. Read-only scan of Slack (public and private channels Billy can see, DMs and group DMs surfaced by search), compared lightly against 19 Oct to 1 Nov. Focus people: Mila, Nils, Mitya, Yehor, Maryna, Roman, Cátia, Tiago, Kate B.

Headline. In these two weeks Casafari moved to make the self-serve AI/MCP platform the default product and sales motion:
  • Token plans went live on 6 Nov.
  • Mila set the goal that "nobody needs a sales call up to ~€1,500/month", and existing contracts move to the platform at renewal.
  • Black Friday was reframed around AI rather than discounts.
  • Staff are pushed toward MCP usage and "proactive personalised outreach".
Running alongside this:
  • Price is being defended on legacy accounts (Sotheby's, "we do not give anything just as granted anymore").
  • Founders are reviewing tool and infra costs.
  • Internal AI agents are spreading fast: agents now post on behalf of Mila, Nils, Tiago, Legal and Maryna.
The friction is concentrated in three places: inbound custom/licensing requests, legal waiting on commercial input, and billing/admin requests handled by hand. All three are good automation candidates.

Method and coverage

  • How it was run: five parallel read-only sweeps (leadership, commercial, product/API, legal/finance, marketing/data). For each key person a from: search covered the window; channels were read from 2 Nov, and substantive threads were opened. The prior-window comparison used light keyword checks only.
  • Authorship: many posts under Mila's and Nils's names are written by their agents. They are treated as founder-sanctioned. Tiago's posts are often agent-signed. Bot-only posts (Themis, Mira, OG-Agent, Billience, monitors) were not counted as human initiatives.
  • Gaps:
    • Partial searches: Kate's and Tiago's from: searches were partly paged, and Mila's last page (around 2 Nov) wasn't read.
    • Threads not opened: the 36-reply #all-hands thread and the 34-reply Black Friday thread.
    • Channels covered by search only: #spain, #portugal, #french-team, #italian_sales, #bi_requests, #marketing_*, #delete_accounts, #tools-support and the country data channels.
    • Links and counts: some rows only have channel-level links. Counts are approximate (±10%). Response times couldn't be measured.
  • Excluded: individual-performance and compensation discussions, by design.
